## Read this before using the numbers

- With `IsEnabled` alone, Chromium 14x and VS Code show only their frames, each with one null
  child: the collector has nothing to read (`chromium reading`: 1 node). Only the forced
  `basic` mode gives a tree (Chromium: 91 shown nodes, complete at depth 24, read in about
  10 ms; VS Code: 12 nodes, no text).
- PSS swings by up to 3x between runs of the same state (Chromium-basic 156 to 452 MiB,
  VS Code 217 to 718 MiB). The process grouping misses Electron and Chromium processes that
  detach and overwrite their environment; the CPU and PSS deltas are not good enough to
  judge the 5 % CPU and 10 % RSS gate. The collector itself stayed under 0.01 % CPU and
  5 MiB PSS in every state.
- Not installed: gnome-text-editor, Kate, Firefox.
